# Product

**What it is.** A private, single-user tool for publishing one video to the owner's own TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube accounts from one screen.

**Who uses it.** One creator, at a desk, usually in the evening, right after finishing an edit. They open it, drop the file, write one caption, pick destinations, press Publish, and glance back later to see whether everything went live.

**Mode.** Operate. The task, the per-platform state and familiar controls outrank expression.

**Jobs.**
1. Upload a video and see it the way followers will (vertical, with the caption over it).
2. Pick destinations and set only the few things each platform requires.
3. Publish once and trust the result: clear per-platform progress, honest failures, one-click retry.
4. Connect accounts without editing files.

**Truths and constraints.**
- Video only. TikTok goes to the user's TikTok inbox as a draft (no public direct posting for personal tools). Instagram needs a public HTTPS address. YouTube needs a title, visibility and a "made for kids" answer.
- Never claim success before the platform confirms it. Demo posts are always marked simulated.
- No passwords, no analytics, no scheduling (yet), no teams.

**Brand commitments (from the owner).** Dark charcoal, no blue. Inspired by the owner's Ethereal Enchantment app (slim sidebar, compact rows, hairline borders). Chosen direction: "Studio console", with a composer next to a phone preview. Neat, compact and user-friendly, but not plain.
